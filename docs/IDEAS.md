# Ideas - Create: Solarpunk

A running list of possible future features. Nothing here is committed. Grouped by theme,
with the strongest / most on-theme ideas near the top of each section.

## Biggest gaps in the renewable lineup

- **Pumped hydro storage** (multiblock) - pump water up on surplus, run it back down through
  a turbine after dark. Pairs directly with solar's day/night cycle.
- **Dedicated grid-scale storage** beyond Heat Battery / Kinetic Battery - flywheel storage
  (a big spinning mass banks SU, very Create-flavored), or a gravity battery that hoists a
  weighted block on surplus power.

## Solar chain depth

- **Parabolic trough** - a linear mid-tier between the Solar Heater and the Solar Power Tower.
- **Stirling dish concentrator** - direct SU/FE from a focused dish, no molten salt loop.
- **Mirror soiling / cleaning** - mirrors lose efficiency over time in desert dust; wash with
  a water spray or a brush to restore output.
- **Molten salt thermocline tank** - a much larger storage multiblock than the Heat Battery,
  slow passive heat loss, keeps a boiler alive through the night.
- **Heat exchanger block** - move heat between molten salt and steam or other fluids.
- **Visible output scaling** - goggle readout of current vs peak output, with rain/snow/night
  penalties shown explicitly.

## Cross-chain synergy (tie salt into the electric chain)

- **Brine evaporation ponds** - low-tech early salt from sun + saltwater, no machine needed.
- **Lithium from brine** - brine to lithium to battery cells, so the salt chain feeds
  panel and battery progression.
- **Panel fabrication chain** - quartz to silicon wafer to doped cell to panel, instead of a
  simple recipe.
- **Fusion fuel cycle** - deuterium from water electrolysis plus tritium bred from lithium,
  linking the salt chain to the Fusion Reactor.

## Biofuel expansion

- **Algae bioreactor** - light + water + CO2 to biomass, an alternative to farming.
- **Anaerobic digester / biogas** - methane from rotten flesh, compost, manure.
- **CO2 loop** - capture Biofuel Engine exhaust and feed it to algae or greenhouses.

## Geothermal

- **Geothermal borehole multiblock** - drill for geothermal anywhere, not just at natural
  vents.
- **Enhanced geothermal** - pump water down, steam comes up, run the Steam Turbine.
- **Uncapped geyser hazard** - scalding damage, eruption timing, a prediction readout.

## Grid management

- **Grid controller** - priority routing, load shedding, comparator output on charge level.
- **Energy monitor block** with an output-over-time graph GUI.
- **Brownout behavior** when demand exceeds supply.

## Solarpunk building / aesthetic

- **Decorative solar** - solar glass, shingles, canopy/pergola, fence.
- **Grid-powered streetlights** that switch on at dusk.
- **Green roofs, living walls, trellises** with climbing plants.
- **Natural building blocks** - rammed earth, bamboo, cork.

## Compat

- **Ad Astra** - strong solar in orbit, weak on Mars, no wind on airless bodies.
